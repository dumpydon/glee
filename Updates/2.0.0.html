Glee 2.0.0

Keep your Mac awake with timer, end-time, or unlimited sessions. Includes display controls, battery limits, and optional closed-lid mode.

This version is free. No account, subscription, or activation key is required.

Closed-lid setup uses macOS Installer and your Mac's administrator approval. Wait for Ready before closing the lid.

Updates are manual in this release. Apple-signed automatic updates are a separate distribution option.
