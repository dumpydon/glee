Your first launch

Open Glee.
Step by step.

This release isn’t notarized by Apple. If you trust your download, approve Glee individually in Privacy & Security.

  1. Download the DMG. Drag Glee into Applications.
  2. For the unverified-app warning, choose Done (or Cancel / OK) and keep the app.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm Open, authenticate on your Mac if asked, then find Glee in your menu bar.

Questions

Before you download.

Is Glee really free?

Yes. This version is free to download and use. There is no account, activation key, subscription, or download fee. The source is available under the AGPL-3.0-only license.

Which Macs does it work on?

Glee requires macOS 14 or later. The same universal download supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Liquid Glass appears on supported systems; macOS 14 and 15 use the native fallback interface.

Why does macOS say “Glee” Not Opened?

This release is locally signed and has not been notarized by Apple. If you trust the download, try opening it, dismiss the warning with Done, then approve Glee in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The 15-second walkthrough shows each step.

How do I keep my Mac awake with the lid closed?

Keep the lid open, connect a charger, and choose Set up… next to Closed-lid mode. Complete the helper installer with your Mac’s administrator approval. Start a session and wait for “Ready to close the lid.” See the closed-lid setup guide. Each Mac needs its own setup.

Can I use closed-lid mode on battery?

Battery use is optional. New installs require a charger by default. Review Battery & Power under General settings, then Closed Lid settings for battery use, cutoff, and time limits. Closed-lid mode has a four-hour continuous cap. Use a ventilated surface and try a short session on your own Mac first.

Does the screen have to stay on?

No. Keep display awake is optional and off by default. With it off, normal display sleep is allowed while Glee prevents system sleep. Display sleep, the screensaver, and computer sleep are separate; Glee does not set their timers or promise a specific closed-lid backlight timeline.

What happens when a two-hour session ends?

Glee releases its sleep prevention and restores its previous closed-lid sleep setting. Your Mac can then sleep according to its normal rules; another app may still prevent sleep. Glee doesn’t terminate your agent. Local work stops progressing while the Mac is asleep, while work running on a remote server can continue there.

Which permissions does Glee need?

Normal sessions need no administrator setup. Optional closed-lid mode installs a privileged helper using macOS Installer and administrator approval. Glee does not require Screen Recording, Accessibility, camera, or microphone access. Read the privacy details.

How do updates and removal work?

Updates are manual in this release. End the session, download the newer app, replace it in Applications, and run closed-lid setup again for that build. To remove Glee, end your session and use Remove setup in Closed Lid settings before moving the app to the Bin. See the install guide.
