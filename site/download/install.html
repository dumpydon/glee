Install Glee

A little setup. A lot of Glee.

Your first session, from download to menu bar.

1. Download Glee for your Mac.

Glee requires macOS 14 or later. One universal app works on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Glee.app into Applications.

↓   Download Glee for macOS

Free to download and use. No account, subscription, or activation key.

2. Approve the first launch, if asked.

Open Glee from Applications. This release is locally signed and has not been notarized by Apple, so macOS may show “Glee” Not Opened or say that Apple could not verify the app.

  1. For that unverified-app warning, choose Done (or Cancel / OK). Keep the app instead of choosing Move to Bin.
  2. If you trust the download, open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway for Glee.
  4. Confirm Open. Authenticate with Touch ID or your Mac login password if requested.

Wording varies by macOS version. If the notice is missing, try opening Glee from Applications again. A managed work or school Mac may restrict these options.

Keep Gatekeeper enabled. For a “damaged” or “will damage your computer” warning, stop and report the problem. You can compare your DMG with the release’s published SHA-256 checksums. An ad-hoc signature is not Apple verification.

Apple’s first-launch guidance ↗

3. Start your first session.

Click the white UFO and puddle symbol in your menu bar. Glee has no Dock icon. Choose Timer, Until, or Unlimited, then select Start session.

New installs require a charger by default. To allow battery use, review Settings → General → Battery & Power. Keep display awake is optional and off by default.

4. Want to close the lid? Set it up once.

First-launch approval and closed-lid setup are separate. Normal sessions need no privileged helper. To use closed-lid mode:

  1. Keep your lid open and connect your charger.
  2. Choose Set up… next to Closed-lid mode.
  3. Complete the helper installation in macOS Installer with your Mac’s administrator approval.
  4. Return to Glee, enable closed-lid mode, and start a session.
  5. Wait for Ready to close the lid, then close your MacBook.

Each Mac needs its own setup. No Apple developer account or paid certificate is needed. If another sleep utility has a closed-lid helper installed, remove its setup through that utility first.

Use a ventilated surface and try a short session on your Mac. Closed-lid use has a four-hour continuous limit, power and battery rules, temperature checks, and a watchdog. Battery use is optional under Closed Lid settings. These limits can end closed-lid protection before the session timer ends.

When your session ends.

Glee releases sleep prevention and restores the previous closed-lid sleep setting. The Mac then follows its normal sleep rules; other apps can still keep it awake. Glee does not kill your processes, monitor your agent, or restart work after sleep. Sessions start manually and do not resume automatically after relaunch or system sleep.

Updates and removal.

Updates are manual in this release. End your session, download the new version from GitHub Releases, and replace Glee in Applications. Run closed-lid setup again after replacing or rebuilding the app to approve that build.

To remove Glee, end your session and open Settings → Closed Lid → Remove closed-lid setup…. Complete the removal, quit Glee, then move the app to the Bin. Removing the app does not automatically erase its local preferences.

Need a hand?

Open a GitHub issue with your Mac model, macOS version, Glee version, and the steps that went wrong. For closed-lid issues, include charger state and the message under Setup details. Keep passwords and personal information out of reports.
