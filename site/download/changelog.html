What’s new in Glee for Mac.

Version 2.0.0 · October 16, 2026

The first Glee release.

A free native menu bar app for keeping your Mac awake while your work continues.

  • Timer, Until, and Unlimited sessions, with quick presets and a 15-minute extension.
  • Optional closed-lid mode with one-time administrator-approved helper setup.
  • Charger, battery cutoff, and closed-lid battery time controls.
  • Temperature checks, a four-hour continuous closed-lid limit, and recovery if contact with the app is lost.
  • Optional display-awake control, remembered duration, and launch at login.
  • A green liquid and UFO app mark, with a white native menu bar symbol.
  • A universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, on macOS 14 or later.
  • Local preferences, no account or activation, and manual updates.

This release is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. See the first-launch guide before installing.
