Privacy

Your work stays yours.

Glee works locally and needs no account.

Local preferences.

Your duration choices, power rules, display preference, and other settings are stored on your Mac in UserDefaults. Glee does not upload your agent conversations, prompts, project files, or session activity.

Power access, with a purpose.

Normal sessions use macOS power assertions. Optional closed-lid mode uses a privileged helper installed with administrator approval. It reads power and battery status, checks thermal state, applies the sleep setting, and restores its previous value when closed-lid protection ends. Local recovery records support that restoration.

Glee does not need Accessibility, Screen Recording, camera, or microphone permission. Launch at login is optional.

No account or added telemetry.

This release has no sign-in, payment flow, activation service, or analytics SDK. It does not inspect your AI accounts or conversations. App updates are manual in this release.

This website and downloads.

Cloudflare hosts this site; GitHub hosts the source and app downloads. Those services receive ordinary web requests when you visit or download, and apply their own privacy practices. This website adds no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, account forms, or tracking cookies.

App panels and the installation walkthrough are illustrations. Their state stays in the page’s memory and disappears when you refresh. They do not change your Mac’s power or security settings. The launch video is hosted here on Cloudflare.

Removing Glee.

End your session and remove closed-lid setup through Glee’s Closed Lid settings before deleting the app. Local preferences are not automatically erased by moving the app to the Bin. See the removal instructions.

Questions or reports.

You can contact the project on GitHub. Issues are public, so omit credentials and private information. Last updated October 16, 2026.
